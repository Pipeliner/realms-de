Anatomy of the ledger
Every vessel the compositor maps is entered into the ledger — a strict ordering of windows per orbit. Layouts never move windows; they project the ledger onto the output's workarea.
Because the projection is pure, undo is trivial: restoring an earlier ledger restores the exact screen, gaps and all.
